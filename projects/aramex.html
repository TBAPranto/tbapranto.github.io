Getting desks ready for the move

The move brought several kinds of work together. I disconnected assigned desk patch cords and removed cameras and an attendance device. At the new office, I worked alongside the team on equipment placement, patching, and reconnection. I also kept up with requests from customers setting up shipping accounts.

What I handled

Workstation connections
I connected Cat6 desk links and compared the outlet labels with recorded ports.
Rack and server equipment
I helped move and reconnect equipment under the IT team’s supervision.
Windows devices and printers
I helped with a Windows 11 and Office 365 laptop preparation assignment. I also operated the multifunction printer and checked print jobs at the workstation.
Customer accounts
I guided customers through registration, email verification, and business account linking.

From the desk to the switch

An outlet label was the link between the cable in my hand and the entry in the port record. I followed the path in these four steps.

Step 1 of 4

Locate the workstation cable

I began at the desk and identified the patch lead connecting the device to its wall outlet.

Step 2 of 4

Read the outlet label

I checked the outlet label against the connection record before tracing the cable at the rack.

Step 3 of 4

Match the panel port

I compared the recorded port with the panel label and flagged incomplete mappings for physical verification.

Step 4 of 4

Record the switch connection

I followed the patch lead to the switch with the IT team and recorded the port or the check still needed.

Helping customers link their accounts

Customers sometimes needed help connecting a new online profile to an existing business shipping account. I guided them through each step, made a reusable screen recording for a recurring procedure, and recorded where to pick up if a request needed follow-up.

Checking a device before handover

For the laptop assignment, I documented Windows and Office setup, licence verification, and the items to check at handover. For printer issues, I used a test copy to check the device itself before looking at the workstation queue and its connection.

What I learned about support

A useful handover identifies the exact point where work stopped. A desk outlet label, a pending licence check, or the last account screen a customer reached gave the next person a clear place to start.
