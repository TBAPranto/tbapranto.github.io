Privacy & credits

Clear sources.Careful presentation.

Local by design

This static site uses local fonts, images, styles and scripts. It has no analytics, advertising, visitor tracking, account system or contact-form backend. External professional-profile and repository links open the relevant third-party services.

Browser storage

The appearance switch stores a light/dark preference in localStorage when available. If storage is unavailable, the switch works for the current page. The site does not store personal visitor information.

Professional information

Content is grounded in the supplied updated CV, internship report, academic research materials and inspected project source. Team-level research results and supervised responsibilities are labelled accordingly. The downloadable CV omits home address, telephone numbers, private email and reference contacts.

Project visuals

The portrait is the supplied photograph, resized and compressed without facial alteration. Asteron XP images are actual local browser captures of the supplied project. Other diagrams and code-style graphics are original conceptual illustrations, not fake application screenshots. Company photographs, internal records and raw research materials are excluded.

Contact

Professional contact is available through the linked LinkedIn and GitHub profiles. Any personal email contact is displayed only if the owner chooses to enable it before publication.
